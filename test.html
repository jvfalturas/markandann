Our Story

⚙️ His Story

I still remember the first time I saw her. I was at a hotel for a conference, and she was behind the reception desk—poised, warm, and utterly captivating. I’m used to blueprints, tolerances, and things that make sense. But she was a beautiful equation I couldn’t solve. I fumbled my room key, and she smiled. That smile? It recalibrated my entire world. From that day, every precision in my life found its purpose: to build a future with her.

🔑 Her Story

Working at the front desk, you meet all kinds of people—but he was different. He walked in with a rolled-up blueprint under his arm and a quiet confidence. When he asked for a late checkout because he was “working on a project,” I couldn’t help but smile. He returned the next week, then the next. One day, he brought me a tiny brass gear he’d machined himself. “For you,” he said. “It’s a small piece of my heart.” I still carry it everywhere. He is my home, my five-star forever.
