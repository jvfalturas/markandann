Our Story

His Story

Mechanical Engineer

I've always believed that the best designs are the ones built on precision — tolerances measured, forces balanced, nothing left to chance. Then I met Marisol, and every calculation I thought I understood fell apart in the best possible way. She walked into a life I'd planned down to the millimeter and rearranged it without asking. I used to think a well-built thing needed a blueprint. She taught me that some of the best things are built one conversation, one late-night phone call, one shared coffee at a time. I still love my blueprints. But now there's only one I'm building for the rest of my life, and her name is at the top of it.

♥

Her Story

Hotel Receptionist

Every day, I welcome strangers and make them feel like they belong somewhere new. I know how to read a room, anticipate what someone needs before they ask, and make the unfamiliar feel like home. Then Aldrich walked up to my desk one afternoon, asking questions that had nothing to do with checking in, and for the first time, I was the one who felt unsteady. He was quiet where I was warm, exact where I was easy-going — and somehow, it worked. He didn't need me to check him in. He needed me to stay. Three years later, I'm still the one greeting people at the door, but now I finally know what it feels like to come home to someone, too.
